crypto-tail-scout-rclone

Personal Google Drive backup client

This OAuth client is used by one owner to back up and restore crypto-tail-scout research datasets between the owner's computer and the owner's private Google Drive using rclone.

Limited access

The client requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can access only the Drive files and folders it creates or that are explicitly opened with it; it cannot browse unrelated Drive content.

No public service

This is not a public application, has no sign-up flow, and has no users other than its owner. It does not sell, advertise with, or disclose Google user data.
